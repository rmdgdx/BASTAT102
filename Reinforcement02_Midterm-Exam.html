STEP 3 OF 3 · GENERAL DIRECTIONS

General directions

  1. This midterm examination has 100 multiple-choice items covering Chapters 5 to 8: estimation and confidence intervals, one-sample hypothesis testing, comparing two groups, and analysis of variance. All items are conceptual; no computation is required. One item is shown per page.
  2. Read each item carefully and choose the best answer from the four options.
  3. Click an option, then confirm with Yes, I'm sure. Once confirmed, your answer is final and the correct answer is shown with a brief explanation.
  4. Your running score (correct answers over the total number of items) is shown in the upper-right corner.
  5. Use Next and Back to move between items. You may skip an item you have not yet confirmed and return to it before you submit.
  6. Each item is worth one point. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so answer every item.
  7. You have 120 minutes. The timer starts when you click Begin examination. When time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically.
  8. Stay on this page until you finish. Switching tabs or windows hides the examination and is recorded.
  9. When you submit, your answers and score are sent to your instructor.
